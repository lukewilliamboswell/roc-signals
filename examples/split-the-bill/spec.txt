# Split the Bill — derived state end to end.
#
# The app owns two source collections: the roster and the ledger. Balances, the
# per-expense shares, and the settlement plan are derived from them. This spec
# drives every interaction and asserts the derived values after each one,
# including the invariant that the net balances always sum to $0.00.
